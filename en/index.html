Services

Four headings for your projects

I offer to put my skills at your service to:

N

Energy modelling

Provide technical assistance on modelling1 of ship energy systems2. Combining rule-based or physics-based models with statistical approaches, I develop custom models or optimise existing ones according to your needs. My assistance can be accompanied by knowledge transfer within your teams.

E

Data analysis

Analyse weather and ship data. To generate value from your data, I:

  • advise stakeholders on the objectives of modelling after data exploration, analysis and processing;
  • create or evaluate your future predictive models3;
  • capitalise on the results through visual narratives4 and summary notes.
S

Content creation

Create content5 in my area of expertise. I can:

  • adapt technical content for different audiences (prospects, customers, students, financiers, media);
  • contribute to the writing process (gathering information, identifying key messages, writing and proofreading);
  • communicate results to specialists or novices using clear illustrations, turning analysis into visual stories;
  • structure and write R&D project proposals for regional, national and European programmes (Interreg, Horizon, AMI CORIMER).
W

Custom training

Provide tailor-made training related to the maritime sector’s energy transition. I can include a variety of teaching methods and training tools, such as face-to-face or online training sessions, synchronous or asynchronous activities, case studies and projects or simulations.

Notes

  1. (1)Numerical modelling, simulation and optimisation at system scale, known as “0D” models. This approach represents the behaviour and interactions of complex multi-physical systems. The complexity of the models depends on the objectives being pursued. ↩
  2. (2)This includes energy converters (internal combustion engine, fuel cell, boiler, recovery boiler, gas turbine, photovoltaic panel, hydro-generator, power take-off/in alternator, wind-assisted propulsion system), storage systems (conventional and alternative fuel tanks and batteries), propellers, combined or hybrid propulsion architectures, on-board HVAC systems, fresh water and refrigeration production, fuel systems… ↩
  3. (3)I mean statistical or machine learning tools that use your historical ship data to predict future events or behaviour. ↩
  4. (4)Line, surface and polar graphics, charts… Static or animated visuals. ↩
  5. (5)Scientific articles for journals, conference proceedings, newsletters, case studies, website pages, procedures, studies and calculation notes, best practices, white papers, reviews of scientific literature, state-of-the-art and technology reviews, application files for calls for R&D projects… ↩
